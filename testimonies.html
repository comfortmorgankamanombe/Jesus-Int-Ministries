Testimonies

What God is doing in our midst

Fruit of the Womb: Mr & Mrs Mapisa

"Our prayer of many years was finally answered. Indeed the Lord has remembered us!"

This was the confession of Mr and Mrs Mapisa with smiles on their faces as they honour Jesus Christ. They had been trying to conceive for 4 years but all their efforts were in vain. After visiting the doctor, they were told she had blocked fallopian tubes, one facing upwards and the other downwards, making fertilisation impossible.

After placing a demand on the anointing, Prophet J Chikambure prayed for them. They returned for a scan and were informed the tubes had been restored to their normal position.

In March 2025, abnormal bleeding began and she was diagnosed with fibroids. They looked again to Jesus Christ for another miracle, and through prayer from Prophet J Chikambure, the fibroid came out and the bleeding completely stopped.

They attended the Living Water Service at JESUS International Ministries in August 2025. The Man of God instructed everyone to approach the altar with only one prayer request. As she approached the altar, she found herself on the floor. They returned home with faith, met as husband and wife, and in September 2025 she missed her period; the pregnancy test was positive.

On the 10th of May 2026, they came to church holding their bouncing baby boy in their arms, returning all Glory and Honour unto Jesus.

The couple's advice: "When challenges come, do not run from God, but rather run to Him."

"He grants the barren woman a home, making her the joyous mother of children. Praise the Lord!" - Psalm 113:9
